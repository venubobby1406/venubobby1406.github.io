The short version of the deal.

ClipSolo is a free tool that runs in your browser. You keep everything you make. Record only what you are allowed to record.

The product, and what it does not come with.

A tool, free, with no account

You get a screen recorder and a small editing bench that run in your browser, at no cost, without signing up. There is no paid tier, no trial and no feature gate, and there is no obligation on you to do anything with us.

No account, no charge

Your content stays yours

What you record, trim and convert is yours. We claim no licence over it, we do not train anything on it, and we do not sell or share it. The processing happens on your device; we never receive the file.

02 — What we ask of you

Two rules, and both of them are about other people's screens.

Record only what you may record

Screen recording can capture other people's work, their messages, their faces. Get permission before you record someone else's screen or camera, and follow the law that applies to you — on privacy, on recording conversations, on work you are shown but do not own.

Your responsibility

Do not use it to break the law

No surveillance, no stalking, no intrusion into a system you are not allowed to touch, and nothing that breaks the law where you are. We do not need to be asked twice, and we do not need to read your file to know: we never receive it.
