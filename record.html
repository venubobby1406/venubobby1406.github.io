03 — Honest limits

What this does not do.

Every recorder has a catch. Ours is that we refuse to put your screen on a server — so here is the honest list instead of a surprise.

No share links yet

A link means somebody else holds the file. We would rather ship nothing than quietly upload your screen, so the file stays yours to send however you like.

Not built

No accounts, no cloud, no tracking

There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to profile. The site has no cookies, no analytics, and no upload endpoint at all.

By design

No AI editing, captions or redaction

Those need a server or a hundred-megabyte model download. Not yet — and we will say so here rather than on a pricing page.

Later, maybe
04 — Straight answers

Questions, answered straight.

If something here is wrong, tell us and we will fix the page and this list at the same time.

Where does the recording go?

Straight to your downloads folder. The file is written by your browser, on your machine — there is no upload step to wait for, and nothing is stored on a server.

Which browsers work?

Chrome, Edge and Brave are the smoothest. Firefox records screen and microphone; system audio depends on what the browser exposes. Safari is limited for recording, and fine for the file tools.

Does it use my data or eat my battery?

No data — nothing is sent anywhere. Battery use is the same as any other screen capture: the encoding costs, not the network.

Can I use it for work?

Yes. Bug reports, tutorials, product demos — anything you are allowed to capture. People use it for client work every day; we just do not take a copy of it.
