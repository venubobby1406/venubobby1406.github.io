02 — First, the honest question

Do you need this at all?

Do you need a whole file at all?

If you're transcribing, most tools take the video directly. If you're quoting one line, you want a clip, not the full track.

Pull the audio when you want the audio: a podcast from a screen share, a voice memo from a webcam clip, a lecture you'll listen to on a walk.

03 — When not to use this

Every tool has a job it is bad at. Here is this one's.

  • you just need to transcribe it → most transcriptions take video directly
  • you need MP3 → this page won't fake it; convert the WAV in an editor that licenses the codec
  • you need one moment of it → trim first, extract less
  • the video has no sound at all → there's nothing to pull, and the bench says so
04 — Questions people actually ask

Asked, answered.

Why WAV and not MP3?
MP3 encoding is patented history that browsers still don't include for free. WAV is what a browser can write without a licensed encoder. We could show a fake "encoding MP3…" spinner — we won't. WAV is lossless and every editor opens it; you choose what to do next.
Does it upload my file?
No. The bench is JavaScript in this tab — your file touches nothing but your own machine.
Is it really free?
Yes — funded by one quiet sponsor slot, never by your files or your data.
