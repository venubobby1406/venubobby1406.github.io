About

Screen recording should be a tab, not a subscription.

We got tired of recorders that make you sign up, install, watermark and time-cap the simplest thing a computer can do: show your screen. So we're building the one we wanted to use.

No install No account No watermark No time limit
01 — The story

Why this exists, how it is built, and what it costs to run.

01 — the problem

Screen recording got gatekept.

Showing someone your screen should be one button. Instead it became an industry: sign-ups before you can press record, watermarks burned into the video, five-minute caps on the free plan, share links that quietly expire, and upgrade prompts parked exactly where the stop button should be.

The tools that respect you exist — OBS, QuickTime, the built-in grabbers — but they're built for a different job: streaming, editing, heavy capture. There was nothing in the gap between "I want to show you this right now" and everything on the market.

02 — what we're building

A recorder that lives in a tab.

No install, no account, no watermark, no time limit. Screen plus a camera bubble that lands exactly where you dragged it. Record a bug, a walkthrough, a demo — hit stop — and get a link the moment you're done.

Sharing needs somewhere to live, so there's honest hosting attached: your link stays up 30 days and you hold the delete token. The recording itself is yours from second one — download it, keep it, do whatever.

03 — house rules

Six rules we don't plan to break.

Opening a tab and pressing record should not require an identity. Accounts will exist later for exactly one optional thing — managing your links. They will never guard the recorder.

Not on the video, not on the player, not "only on the free plan". Your recording is not our billboard.

A three-hour recording is your business, not our upsell trigger. The stop button is the only thing that ends a recording.

No fake countdowns, no "your video expires — upgrade!" emails, no upgrade prompt jammed under the stop button. Free means free.

The little floating circle — drag it anywhere, it lands exactly there in the video. In the free plan. Obviously.

One sponsor slot pays the bills. We don't sell viewer insights, we don't train anything on recordings, and there's no "we may share with partners" clause — because there is no clause.

04 — how it stays free

Honest math, not a paywall.

Playback needs storage, and storage costs money — so here's the deal instead of a pricing page: links live 30 days, then the bytes are recycled. That one rule keeps storage bounded and hosting boring-cheap, cheap enough that a single sponsor slot on the landing page covers it.

The recorder, the tools, the links: free forever. If money ever enters the picture it will be for heavy storage or teams — never as a hostage on basics.

05 — where we are

The roadmap, unfiltered.

Browser tab, unlimited length, camera bubble, dual versions (with camera / screen only), delete token. Being used and tested daily.

A link that's ready the moment you stop recording. Cloudflare Worker + storage, 30-day lifecycle, private delete token.

Screenshot, webcam and voice recorders, plus the video workbench — trim, convert, compress, GIF and audio extract, all in the tab. Nothing uploaded, ever.

See and manage your links from one place. Optional forever — never required to record.

The browser is the point. Nothing to install, nothing to update, nothing to uninstall.
