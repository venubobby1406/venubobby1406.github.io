About

Screen recording should be a tab, not a subscription.

Recorders that make you sign up, install, watermark and time-cap the simplest thing a computer can do. We built the one we wanted to use.

No install No account No watermark No time limit
01 — The story

Why this exists, what it is, and what it costs to run.

01 — the problem

Screen recording got gatekept.

Showing someone your screen should be one button. Instead it became an industry: sign-ups before you can press record, watermarks burned into the video, five-minute caps on the free plan, and upgrade prompts parked where the stop button should be.

OBS and QuickTime are good, but they are built for streaming and heavy capture. Nothing sat in the gap between "I want to show you this right now" and everything on the market.

02 — what we're building

A recorder that lives in a tab.

No install, no account, no watermark, no time limit, and a camera bubble that lands exactly where you dragged it. Record a bug, a walkthrough, a demo — hit stop, and the file is on your disk.

The recording is yours from the first second — download it, keep it, send it however you like. What we do not do is sit on it.

03 — how it stays free

Honest math, not a paywall.

Recording costs us nothing: the browser does the work on your machine. Hosting costs money, so the deal is a single sponsor slot on the landing page, and — if shared links ever ship — storage with an expiry.

The recorder and the tools are free. If money ever enters the picture it will be for heavy storage or teams — never as a hostage on basics.

The recorder is right there.

No download, no account, no watermark. That is the whole point.
