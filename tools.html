04 — Straight answers

If something here is wrong, tell us and we will fix the page and this list at the same time.

Do these tools upload my file?

No. Every conversion happens in your browser with WebCodecs and WebAssembly — the file is read from your disk and written back to it. There is no server involved, which is also why there is no upload bar and no “processing your video” wait.

Why is the trim re-encoded?

A browser cannot cut a container without rewriting it. We re-encode as it plays, in real time, and keep your original file untouched — so you can always trim the original again instead of trimming the trimmed copy.

How large a file can I drop?

As large as your browser can decode. Very large files work but take as long as the render takes — a 30-second cut takes about 30 seconds, because it is really being recorded again.

Is there a paid version?

No. There is no paid tier, no trial and no feature gate. If this ever needs a server, we will say so here before we ask you for anything.
