04 — What will never happen

Every refusal has a reason, and none of the reasons are "roadmap".

  • AVI → MP4

    Your browser can't decode 20-year-old codecs — and we won't fake a progress bar over a failure.

  • OS-wide auto-zoom

    A browser can't see your clicks outside its own tab. That's why Cap is a native app — and why we won't cosplay as one.

  • Server-side anything

    Unlimited cloud storage, team workspaces, viewer analytics — all of it needs a server we run, holding your files and your viewers' behavior. Storage costs money forever, accounts mean identities and permissions, and analytics means watching. Hard no by design; your files stay yours.

  • AI titles & summaries

    Needs GPU servers. The day that's free to run, it'll be here. Until then — no theater.

  • Native apps

    You already have one — it's called a browser. Phone screen recording is a different machine, so we're a desktop-browser tool, and we say so.

  • 4K theater

    We capture your screen the size it is. No upscaling, no "up to 4K" asterisks.

05 — Six promises

None of them are "coming soon".

01

No account to record.

Opening a tab and pressing record should not require an identity. Accounts will exist later for exactly one optional thing — managing your links. They will never guard the recorder.

02

No watermark. Anywhere.

Not on the video, not on the player, not "only on the free plan". Your recording is not our billboard.

03

No time limits.

A three-hour recording is your business, not our upsell trigger. The stop button is the only thing that ends a recording.

04

No dark patterns.

No fake countdowns, no "your video expires — upgrade!" emails, no upgrade prompt jammed under the stop button. Free means free.

05

The camera bubble is included.

The little floating circle — drag it anywhere, it lands exactly there in the video. In the free plan. Obviously.

06

Your data isn't the price.

One sponsor slot pays the bills. We don't sell viewer insights, we don't train anything on recordings, and there's no "we may share with partners" clause — because there is no clause.

Nothing on this page is a promise about a date.

It is a list of what exists, what is next, and what will not. If it is out of date, tell us and we will fix it.
